# a_balance_mod








## What this mod is
This mod is a combination of some buffs for underwhelming/bad cards and some reworks.

## The balance philosophy
My balance philosophy mainly consists of 2 things:
1. There is allowed to be bad cards in the game, but those cards should have some kind of niche where they are either good or fun to play with, preferably both. Also, units you rarely see are allowed to be a bit more powerful if their use can help differ the run from the norm.
2. I don't want to change the game too much. There are several cards that I would like to rework(cave in for example), but I have tried to keep the changes somewhat limited for the most part.

## The changes
A reminder that the things said as reasons for changing cards are all just my personal opinions. It's very possible that you disagree with me and think that, for example, Shadowsiege is the best unit ever. I am open to feedback, just keep it civil

#### Banished:
Battle Dancer: Valor on shift 3 -> 4
I often find Battle Dancer to be quite underwhelming. It is one of only 2 non-rare units that utilise shift, and it's the main one to do so, but the payoff is often not quite at the level it needs to be. As such, I thought I would make it a bit better, both as just a unit, and as a core part of shift playstyles.


#### Pyreborn:
Pyreblooded: Attack 5 -> 10 and Health 10 -> 15. Ability cooldown 3 -> 2
I think we can all agree that Pyreblooded is a bad unit. Pyreblooded is one of those cards I wasn't sure if I should let it be because I would rather rework it, or if I should just give it some buff. Some buff it is. The main part of the buff is the cooldown reduction. Being able to use the ability every 2nd turn should allow it to scale much more reliably. The stat increases are just a bonus because I felt like it needed them


#### Luna Coven:
Arduhn, Mystic Hammer 1: Health 25 -> 30
Arduhn, Mystic Hammer 2: Health 45 -> 60
These changes are made to make Mystic Hammer fit better into the category of a somewhat safe champion path; One with a high floor, but low ceiling

Scrivener: Ability cooldown 2 -> 1



#### Underlegion:
Bolete, Headsman 2: Attack 60 -> 80
This Bolete path's attack normally goes 30 -> 60 -> 160. I find that I very rarely go all in on headsman, however, because of the power of Bolete's other paths. I hope that a better 2nd level will somewhat alleviate that

#### Hellhorned:
Shardtail Queen, Royalty 2: Attack 15 -> 10. Attack per rally: 15 -> 10. Added multistrike
Shardtail Queen, Royalty 3: Attack 25 -> 35. Attack per rally: 25 -> 15. Added multistrike
The Royalty path as it is, is quite underwhelming when not paired with Melting Remnant or Underlegion. You kinda need to find Onehorn's Tome to give her multistrike for her to be effective. So, I reduced her scaling a bit and gave her multistrike. She should now deal more damage while also wasting less damage on overkilling enemies

Consumer of Crowns: Ability cooldown 2 -> 0(can be used multiple times per turn)
This change was made because I think that the idea of Consumer of Crown's ability; That it can help clear space on the floor while also getting a big attack in. Unfortunately, it is just not that great with a cost of 4, space of 3, and cooldown of 2. So I decided to changed the cooldown to keep it as a unit that is usually bad, but if you can provide it with enough imps, it can(theoretically) kill anything in one turn. I love strategies that are different from the norm, and this can enable such a strategy


#### Awoken:
Wyldenten, Strangler 2: Added effect: "Slay: Apply Regen 3 to friendly units"
Wyldenten, Strangler 3: Added effect: "Slay: Apply Regen 6 to friendly units"
Strangler is a weird path, where the first level is very powerful when combined with other paths, but level 2 and 3 only add stats, and not even good stats either. Even with these changes, I think going Strangler 3 is gonna be rare, but at least there is a reason to take Strangler 2

Rootseeds: Added effect: "Restore 1 health"
I am tired of exiled awoken not being able to use hollows at all. This is still a much worse heal than restore, but it's enough to reliably activate rejuvenate triggers

Cycle of Life: Cost 3 -> 2.
I debated for a while if I wanted to increase the effect, reduce the cost, or maybe add a heal to the card. I ended up reducing the cost to keep the change somewhat simple. I could easily be persuaded to do one of the other things instead depending on the feedback I get

Animus of Will: Added effect: "Slay: Cultivate 2"
Awoken has 2 sweeper units; Animus of Will is not one of them. On top of having sweep, those 2 units also have innate ways to scale, so I thought it was only fair to give Animus of Will a way to scale too.


#### Stygian Guard:
Solgard, Coldchannel 1: Health 25 -> 30
Solgard, Coldchannel 2: Health 50 -> 60
Solgard, Coldchannel 3: Health 70 -> 80
Coldchannel has a very high ceiling, but also a very low floor for effectiveness. You really need to be able to support it with your allied clan since Stygian itself has few ways of helping Solgard survive. These health increases are not gonna change a whole lot, but shoud make the path just a bit more viable when you find yourself allied with a clan that can help him

Hoarfrost Effigy: Cost 3 -> 2
Hoarfrost Effigy is a card that was bad in the first game and worse in the second. I would have liked to remove the card and add something else instead, but that is outside the scope of this mod, so I have instead simply reduced the cost

Ice and Pyre: Added Permafrost
Ice and Pyre seems to be made to pick off single enemies that are about to reach your pyre. Unfortunately you can normally be sure to draw it on the turn that you need it, so I gave it permafrost.

Guard of the Unnamed: Added effect: "Incant: Apply Armor 1 to friendly units"
Guard of the Unnamed needs its own niche since it is too close to just being a worse Siren of the Sea. Now it works better as a way to also defend your backline in fights where that is necessary.

Eel Gorgon: Attack 10 -> 20 and Health 5 -> 20
Eel gorgon takes a lot of work to make good. I think after these changes the unit is still bad, but it needs slightly less than before to become good; Also, the reason I buffed its health is for it to survive against Qel and corruption Seraph without health upgrades


#### # Umbra:
Penumbra, Monstrous 1: Health 30 -> 35
Penumbra, Monstrous 2: Attack 125 -> 150 and Health 60 -> 80
Penumbra, Monstrous 3: Attack 250 -> 350 and Health 120 -> 150
I find that Penumbra doesn't really fulfill his role of being a big unit that you don't need to support all that much. These extra stats should be able to help him either pick off units that didn't die on lower floors, or get some big hits in against flying bosses

Penumbra, Architect 1: Attack 25 -> 20. Added effect: "Hunger: +3 Attack"
Penumbra, Architect 2: Health 30 -> 25. Added effect: "Hunger: +6 Attack"
Penumbra, Architect 3: Attack 100 -> 90 and Health 40 -> 35. Added effect: "Hunger: +10 Attack"
I prefered Architect Penumbra in MT1, but I find that his quality of being "free" in terms of space just isn't enough in MT2. To change it, I decided to give it a bit more of an identity as a backling damage dealer, something that Umbra lacks. He should work well standing behind a gorger, dealing damage, and not taking up space(post ring 3).

Void Binding: Emberdrain 3 -> 1
Void Binding is a card that is good if you have perils of production, but unusable otherwise. With the emberdrain reduction, this can realistically serve as a defensive pick, even for runs that aren't using Perils

Overgorger: Added effect: "Gorge: +1 health permanently"

Shadowsiege: Cost 6 -> 5, Size 6 -> 5, Attack 200 -> 300, and Health 150 -> 250
Shadowsiege is... Really bad. I was tempted to do some kind of rework, where it was cheaper but with emberdrain on it, but to be honest I like the idea behind it as it is; It just doesn't have the stats needed to be the big unwieldly angel-killer that it wants to be, so I decided to just make it slightly better in all aspects so that it is easier to make work and has a higher payoff

#### Melting Remnant:
Rector Flicker, Burnbright 2: Attack and health 100 -> 120
Rector Flicker, Burnbright 3: Attack and health 200 -> 250
This path gives you a big body, but no scaling or utility. He doesn't kill heavies in one blow, but can be a big help in clearing them; I just find that he is often not quite as helpful as I'd like him to be outside of the early game.

Rector Flicker, Accumulator 1: Attack from harvest 1 -> 2
Rector Flicker, Accumulator 2: Attack from harvest 2 -> 3
Rector Flicker, Accumulator 3: Attack from harvest 4 -> 6
This path is meant to be a tank, but I often find that Wickless Baron is better because of his ability to also scale attack, so I changed Accumulator to keep its tankiness, but end up with slightly more damage scaling than baron has without upgrades.

Hallowed Halls: Cost 3 -> 2
I have heard some people say that the nerf to Hallowed Halls was needed, and have heard others say that it wasn't even that good before. All I know for sure is that, when the cost was increased to 3, the card became a lot less *fun* for me. As such, I am returning it to 2 cost

Draff: Cost 1 -> 0

Bounty Stalker: Attack 10 -> 16. Attack gain on extinguish 8 -> 16
Bounty Stalker just doesn't scale fast enough for MT2. I tried changing it to just +12, but it was still underwhelming, and when it comes to rare units, I would rather they are a little too strong than a little too weak since you see them so rarely.

#### Neutral:
Dante's inferno: Cost 2 -> 1
I almost never take Dante's inferno; Not because it is a bad card, but because the relic is very good. I wanted to give a minor buff to Dante's inferno to make it more viable as a fun choice
